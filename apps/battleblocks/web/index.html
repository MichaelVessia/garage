Quick rules

Win three stars to win the set.

→ Hop
Pick a card. Count that many spaces. Move either way.
Push
Stand beside your buddy to push them back.
Strike
Match the gap with your card. Play extra copies for a stronger strike.
Dash and strike
Add two cards to reach your buddy. Hop with the first and strike with the second.
◎ Block
Match every strike card to block. An open hit wins the round. After a dash, you can also hop back and miss your next turn.

Keys 1–5 pick a card. Escape clears it. Enter plays a single action.
